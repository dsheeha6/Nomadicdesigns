About

<Nomadic Designs> is a solo-built creative studio — founded by Danny Sheehan — focused on helping small businesses and startups thrive online.

Through custom websites, conversion-driven landing pages, and smart automations powered by AI, I help entrepreneurs and teams work smarter, save time, and grow their presence from anywhere in the world.

Beyond client projects, I build and experiment with tools like PromptPilot, Savu, RealityCheck, and Missed2Booked — combining design, automation, and data insight to solve real-world problems.

Driven by a passion for design, technology, and data, I'm currently expanding my skill set into analytics — learning tools like Tableau, SQL, and Python to better measure, visualize, and optimize how digital systems perform.

Whether I'm collaborating with clients or joining a team full-time, my goal stays the same: to design systems and experiences that make business simple, beautiful, and scalable.
